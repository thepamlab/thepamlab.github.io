Prof. Chang will be representing the lab at the upcoming <a href="https://interspeech2026.org/" target="_blank">Interspeech 2026</a> conference in Sydney!

The title of his talk (co-authored with Meixian Li and Prof. Yao Yao) is "A corpus-based study of creaky voice production in English and Mandarin". It will be the last talk (10:40-11:00) in the Tuesday morning oral session on "Voice Quality Aspects of Speech".

Congratulations to all!
