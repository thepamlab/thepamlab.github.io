Welcome to the three new students joining the lab this semester:
<ul>
 	<li><b>Cheryl Chan</b> (CityU '26) is a recent graduate of the BA in Linguistics and Language Applications at City University of Hong Kong. She will be a research intern in the lab, working with PhD alum Jupitara Ray. Her research interests include the experimental study of tone and speech perception, as well as the acoustic and prosodic aspects of meaning-making in naturalistic discourse.</li>
 	<li><b>Zhuangcheng Gao</b> (CityU '30) is a first-year PhD student in Linguistics at City University of Hong Kong. A graduate of Hunan Normal University and Wuhan University, he is broadly interested in phonetics and phonology, pragmatics, bilingualism, cognitive linguistics, and sociolinguistics. Currently, he is working on aspects of code-switching between Mandarin and English.</li>
 	<li><b>Tianqi Ju</b> (PolyU '30) a first-year PhD student in Language Science and Technology at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, co-supervised by Profs. Yao Yao and Charles Chang. Her previous work looked at cross-linguistic influence in Chinese multilingual speakers, with a focus on both speech production and perception. Her research interests include phonetics, multilingualism, and language acquisition.</li>
</ul>
