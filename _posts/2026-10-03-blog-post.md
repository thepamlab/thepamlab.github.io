Prof. Chang will be in Porto Alegre, Brazil next week to give a short course on language attrition at the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS) and a plenary address at the <a href="https://www.even3.com.br/icla5/" target="_blank">5th International Conference on Language Attrition and Bilingualism</a> (ICLA 5)! The title of his plenary is "Drift and attrition as a laboratory for testing perception–production links".
